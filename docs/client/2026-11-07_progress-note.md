Hi Rehana,

Here is your progress note. It is later than the fortnight we promised, and we
are sorry about that. From here on it will be every two weeks.

## What is new

The first public part of your new website is built, in the Fabric Fun style
you chose. Visitors can now:

- **See all six classes** on the home page, with your photos, prices and the
  next date for each.
- **Filter the classes**: in the studio, online, or kids and parents.
- **Open a class page** with the price, how long it runs, where it is, the
  upcoming dates and how many places are left on each.
- **Browse a calendar**, a month at a time, of every class date.
- **Send you a message** from a contact page. That includes private event
  enquiries, which stay offline as we agreed.

Each class page also sets out your cancellation policy, in your own terms, so
people see it before they book.

Online booking and payment are the next stage. Until then, every date has an
"Ask about this date" button, which opens the contact form with the class and
date already filled in.

We also checked every page on a phone and on a computer: the text size, how
clearly it reads against the background, and that every button is easy to tap.

card, or a credit voucher for the rest? For now the class pages only mention
the $15 fee.

**2. The "Hello, I'm Rehana" section on the home page.** We wrote it from what
you have told us: twenty years in textiles, as a fashion designer and a
university lecturer, and the only artist teaching Indonesian batik in the
Adelaide area. Please read it and change anything that is not how you would
say it.

**3. Longer class descriptions, if you have them.** At the moment each class
page uses the short description from Eventbrite. If you would like more on
each page, for example what people make, what to wear or what to bring, send
it over. Otherwise you will be able to add it yourself later from your
dashboard.

And one to confirm: we have set the Kids and Parents class so that **$42 covers
one child and their adult together**, and the number of places counts children.
Tell us if that is not right.
