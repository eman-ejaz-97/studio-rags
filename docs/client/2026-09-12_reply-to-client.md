## 2. Private events will stay off the website

We have decided that private events — corporate groups, hens parties and
birthdays — will not be booked through the website. They will stay exactly as
you run them now, by phone and email, the same as your council and school
bookings.

The reason is your deposit. Private events take a $200 deposit up front with the
balance due later, while every other class on your site is paid in full at the
time of booking. Those are two quite different payment systems, and building the
second one would take significant time away from the main booking journey.

What the website will do for private events:

- A page explaining what you offer for private events
- Your terms, including the minimum of 10 guests and the travel surcharge
- An enquiry form that emails you the details, so you can quote and invoice as
  you do today

The travel surcharge ($50 for 12–20 km, $75 for 20–35 km) will be published on
that page for the same reason.

If you would rather private events were bookable online later, we can look at it
after launch. We just do not want it slowing down the part of the site that
brings in most of your bookings.

## 3. Something we need to flag about gift cards

Your policy says gift cards bought on the website are valid for **12 months**.

Our understanding is that Australian Consumer Law requires gift cards sold to
customers to be valid for **at least three years** from the date of purchase.

We are not lawyers and we do not want to overstep, so please check this with your
accountant or a business adviser. But we did not want to build something into
your website that might cause you a problem later, without saying anything.

For now we have set the system to three years. It is a single setting and easy to
change if your adviser tells you otherwise.

Your **credit vouchers** — the ones you offer instead of a refund — look like a
different thing to a gift card that someone buys, so the six-month validity on
those is likely fine. Worth asking about both while you are there.

## 4. Your refund policy — we are building it exactly as written

We are implementing your existing policy rather than inventing a new one. For the
record, this is how the website will behave:

- **More than 14 business days before the class** — full refund to the original
  payment method, or a credit voucher valid 6 months. The customer chooses.
- **14 business days or less** — a $15 per person fee applies.
- **No show** — no refund, no voucher, no reschedule.
- **If you cancel a session** — the customer gets a full refund, a reschedule or
  a gift card, whichever they prefer.

All of this will be published on the website so customers see it before they pay.

You will also be able to override any of it. If you want to be generous with a
particular customer, you can. The policy is the default, not a lock.
