## 2. The domain name — our recommendation

We recommend you register:

**studiorags.com.au**

Why this one:

- **`.com.au` is reserved for registered Australian businesses.** You need an ABN
  to get one, which you have. That restriction is exactly what makes customers
  trust it. Anyone can buy a `.com`. A `.com.au` tells an Adelaide customer they
  are dealing with a real local business.
- It also helps you appear in Google searches made in Australia.
- It is affordable — roughly **$20 to $35 per year**. We suggest registering for
  two or three years at once, so there is no risk of it lapsing.

Where to buy it: an Australian registrar such as **VentraIP** or **Netregistry**.

At checkout they will offer you extras — website hosting, email hosting, website
builders, SEO packages. **Please decline all of them.** You only need the domain
name itself. We are providing everything else as part of the project.

**One important point.** Please register the domain in **your own name, under
Studio Rags' ABN, with your own email address** as the contact. Not ours. Not the
university's.

The domain is your business's address. It should belong to you permanently, long
after our project finishes. You should be the only person who controls it.

We are happy to join a call and walk you through the purchase screen by screen,
but the account itself should be yours.

We have asked our supervisor to review this recommendation. Their details are at
the end of this email, so you can confirm it with them directly.

## 3. Your Canva fonts — leave this one with us

There is a catch with Canva fonts worth knowing about. Fonts inside Canva are
licensed for use *within Canva*. That licence usually does not extend to putting
the font file on a public website. So simply downloading it is not always the
correct step.

The good news is that many of Canva's fonts are freely available elsewhere. In
most cases we can use your exact font on the website at no cost.

**All we need from you is the font names.** In Canva, open your Brand Kit, take a
screenshot of the fonts section, and email it to us. That is all. We will handle
the rest.

If one of them does need a paid licence for website use, we will tell you what it
costs and offer you a very similar free alternative to choose from.
